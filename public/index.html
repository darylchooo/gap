Gastrointestinal Animated Pictograms (GAP) Survey

Thank you for helping us.

Terima kasih kerana membantu kami.

You will see a series of pictures showing different stomach or bowel symptoms.

Anda akan melihat beberapa gambar yang menunjukkan simptom perut atau usus.

Please look at each picture carefully and answer the questions.

Sila lihat setiap gambar dengan teliti dan jawab soalan yang diberikan.

There are no right or wrong answers.

Tiada jawapan betul atau salah.

We only want to know what you think and feel.

Kami hanya ingin mengetahui pendapat dan pengalaman anda.

Symptoms

1. Burning sensation in the oesaphagus, belching and regurgitation of acid into the oesaphagus (tube joining mouth and stomach)

2. Dysphagia (difficulty swallowing)

3. Fullness (feeling of stomach filled to capacity without relation to prior food intake)

4. Early Satiety (stomach is overfilled soon after starting to eat, disproportionate to the quantity of food taken, so that food cannot be finished)

5. Post-prandial Pain or discomfort (upper abdominal symptoms start or get worse after meals)

6. Epigastric pain / upper abdominal pain (pain between bottom edge of rib cage, below the sternum)

7. Retrosternal discomfort (unpleasant feeling behind the chest bone)

8. Pain or discomfort prior to defecation

9. Difficulty defecating (straining or incomplete evacuation of the bowel)

10. Constipation (reduced frequency of bowel movements, hard and lumpy stool)

11. Loose stool (soft or liquid stool)

12. Incontinence (inability to control stool)

13. Urgency to defecate

14. Diarrhea (increased in stool frequency, frequently associated with watery or loose stool)

15. Loss of appetite (not feeling hungry)

16. Abdominal cramps (spasmodic or colic like stomach pain without specified localisation)

17. Sickness (the feeling you have before you need to vomit)

18. Nausea (urgent feeling of the need to vomit)

19. Vomiting (vomiting of mucus and / or gastric contents / food, or strong unproductive retching)

20. Bloating (feeling of swelling of abdomen and excessive gas in the abdomen)

21. Excessive gas and flatulence

22. Excessive Belching (without acid)

23. Anxiety

24. Depression
